# GreenFarmify
We transform climate data and soil characteristics into smart agricultural decisions, helping farmers anticipate weather conditions, protect their crops, and preserve the fertility of their land.



# About the Project

## Problem Statement

Climate change has altered rainfall patterns and agricultural seasons, causing traditional planting and harvesting calendars to no longer align with current environmental conditions. This creates uncertainty for farmers, increases the risk of crop losses, and often leads them to rely on fertilizers and other agricultural inputs to compensate for unfavorable conditions. However, when these products are used without considering soil characteristics, they can contribute to soil degradation and loss of fertility.

Nevertheless, the problem is not the lack of information. Today, there are numerous sources of climate data, statistics, and tools available to monitor environmental and agricultural conditions. The real challenge is that this information is scattered across different sources and is not always easy to interpret or apply to agricultural decision-making.

**Because having information is not enough if we cannot turn it into useful decisions.**

## Our Solution

We are developing a platform that integrates and transforms satellite climate data and information collected through ground-based sensors into clear, accessible agricultural recommendations tailored to the conditions of each region.

Through an interactive interface, farmers can access information about selected municipalities in Yucatán, visualize precipitation, temperature, and humidity calendars, understand the conditions of their land, and receive strategic plans that include planting seasons, crop selection, and crop rotation recommendations.

Our proposal does not seek to replace farmers' traditional knowledge or working methods, but rather to complement them with organized information and probabilistic analysis that help them make more informed and confident decisions.

**We don't replace your methods; we help you improve your decisions.**

## Impact and Value Proposition

Our goal is to bridge the gap between information availability and its practical application in agriculture. Our value proposition is to bring different sources of information together in one place, make them easier to understand, and transform them into strategies that address the specific needs of each plot of land.

Through this approach, we aim to reduce uncertainty in agricultural planning, minimize the risk of crop losses, and encourage sustainable practices such as crop rotation, which contributes to maintaining soil fertility and health.

Our vision is to provide farmers with a tool that helps them better understand their environment, anticipate changing climate conditions, and make the most of available information without losing the value of their own experience.

**We don't aim to generate more information; we aim to give meaning to the information that already exists and turn it into better agricultural decisions.**
